Direction A

The canon, drawn

the seven, joined in birth order
wrote the most, read the least
everyone else — size is monthly readers

What it says. Keeps the historical spine and spends hue on the argument instead of on lifespan. The seven are joined in birth order, so the line is the claim: Richter to Shostakovich, two centuries in one gesture. Ringed in blue, the composers who wrote the most and are read the least — Cambini at 149 quartets and 216 readers a month, Ellerton at 100 and 53.

What it costs. Readership is still only circle size, so “Bartók is read 76× more per quartet than Cambini” is asserted, not shown — the eye cannot measure it off two circles. It is the smallest change to what ships, and the weakest form of the argument.
